Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an AI project typically take? +
Most focused AI projects take 6–12 weeks from discovery to production deployment. Larger enterprise implementations may take 3–6 months. We always start with a 2-week discovery sprint to give you a precise timeline before committing to a full engagement.
Do we need large amounts of data to get started? +
Not always. We assess your data during discovery and recommend the right approach — whether that is fine-tuning with your data, using foundation models with RAG, or collecting new data. Many powerful AI solutions can be built with surprisingly small datasets.
How do you ensure AI model accuracy and reliability? +
We implement rigorous testing, validation, and monitoring pipelines. Every model goes through offline evaluation, A/B testing, and gradual rollout before full production deployment. We also set up continuous monitoring to detect drift and retrain as needed.
Can you integrate AI with our existing systems? +
Yes — integration is core to what we do. We design AI solutions to work with your existing infrastructure via APIs, webhooks, and native integrations. We have integrated with Salesforce, SAP, custom databases, and virtually every major enterprise system.
What ongoing support do you provide post-launch? +
We offer flexible support packages including model monitoring, retraining, performance optimization, and feature additions. Most clients stay with us on a retainer after initial delivery.
