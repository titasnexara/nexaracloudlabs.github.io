The Founding Story

Why Nexara Cloud Labs exists

Nexara Cloud Labs was founded by Titas, a technology leader with 13 years of hands-on IT experience spanning AI, data engineering, full-stack development, and quality engineering.

Over more than a decade, Titas built deep expertise working with clients across Europe and beyond — earning a reputation for technical rigour, transparent communication, and delivery that actually sticks. When she launched Nexara Cloud Labs, the mission was clear: bring that same standard of engineering excellence to a wider range of businesses, without the overhead of a large consultancy.

We are early-stage by choice — lean, focused, and deeply invested in every project we take on. We would rather do fewer things exceptionally well than scale prematurely and dilute quality.

🎯

Our Hiring Philosophy

The standard every engineer must meet

The technology industry has a body-shopping problem. CVs get forwarded, generalists get placed as specialists, and clients pay enterprise rates for junior output. We built Nexara specifically to fix that.

Every engineer we place goes through a technical interview conducted by a domain practitioner — an AI engineer interviews AI engineers, a QA lead interviews QA leads, a data engineer interviews data engineers. No shortcuts, no rubber stamps, no exceptions.
